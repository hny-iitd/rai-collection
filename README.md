I work on **Responsible AI**: measuring and reducing the harm machine-learning systems can cause in
high-stakes domains such as **finance** and **healthcare**.

### Responsible AI collection

| Repository | Focus | Highlights |
|---|---|---|
| [**rai-metrics-core**](https://github.com/hny-iitd/rai-metrics-core) | Metric library | 30+ fairness, transparency, robustness & privacy metrics · mitigation · model cards |
| [**rai-finance-credit-fairness**](https://github.com/hny-iitd/rai-finance-credit-fairness) | Finance | Disparate impact & four-fifths rule · redlining-proxy detection · adverse-action reasons · fairness-vs-profit trade-off |
| [**rai-healthcare-clinical-fairness**](https://github.com/hny-iitd/rai-healthcare-clinical-fairness) | Healthcare | Under-diagnosis bias · missed patients per 10k · cost-as-proxy (Obermeyer 2019) · k-anonymity & membership inference |

### What I measure

- **Fairness:** demographic parity, disparate impact, equal opportunity, equalized odds, calibration, intersectional audits
- **Transparency:** proxy-feature scans, permutation importance, reason codes, model cards
- **Robustness:** noise sensitivity, decision flip rates, drift (PSI)
- **Privacy:** k-anonymity, l-diversity, membership-inference risk, differential privacy
- **Impact:** turning metrics into outcomes people care about: credit wrongly denied, patients missed, profit and risk
